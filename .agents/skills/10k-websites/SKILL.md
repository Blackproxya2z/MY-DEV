# 10K Websites — Cinematic Scroll-Driven Website Standard

Build a cinematic scroll-driven website: a full-bleed hero canvas/video plays forward as the visitor scrolls down and backward as they scroll up, choreographed captions and story beats unfold in the negative space over radial contrast scrims, and the page settles into a luxury website below with real sections, human copy, one mid-journey interactive moment, and a clear call to action.
